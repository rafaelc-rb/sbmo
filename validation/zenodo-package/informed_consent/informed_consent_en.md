# Informed Consent (ICF), English translation

*This is a translation of the verified Portuguese original
(`informed_consent_pt.md`), which is a verbatim transcription of the real
form actually used to collect the 31 responses
(`https://forms.gle/sccnD9vJSvMVXcoj9`), confirmed against a PDF export of
the live form (2026-08-18). No evidence was found that the English-language
variant of the form (generated by `sbmo_create_individual_form.gs`, which
supports both a Portuguese and an English form) was ever actually deployed
or used: all 31 raw responses use Portuguese field values, so only the
Portuguese form is confirmed as the real data-collection instrument. Treat
this file as a translation for international readers, not as independently
verified original text.*

## Form description

This questionnaire is part of a master's thesis at PPGES/UNIPAMPA and
supports validating the Software Business Model Ontology (SBMO). Responses
are confidential and used exclusively for academic purposes.

The research targets people with practical knowledge of a software product,
such as founders, CEOs, product leaders, business leaders, or professionals
with a clear view of how the software generates revenue, delivers value,
reaches customers, and positions itself in the market.

The Informed Consent form appears at the start of the questionnaire,
followed by the research questions. Estimated time: 15 to 20 minutes.

## Informed Consent Form (ICF)

You are invited to take part in research associated with a master's thesis
at the Postgraduate Program in Software Engineering (PPGES), Federal
University of Pampa (UNIPAMPA), with the goal of collecting information
about software business models to validate the Software Business Model
Ontology (SBMO).

**Procedures**: participation consists of completing this form (estimated
time: 15-20 minutes). If e-mail is collected, it is for academic contact,
per Google Forms settings.

**Risks and Discomforts**: every measure will be taken during data
collection to protect your privacy and anonymity. No risks or discomfort
are otherwise expected during the study beyond common issues such as
fatigue, stress, or mild discomfort.

**Benefits**: the research may help advance ontological descriptions of
software business models. This study will also produce results relevant to
the LESSE research group. You will incur no costs to take part, nor
receive any reimbursement or compensation for authorizing the use of your
data.

**Confidentiality**: responses are treated as confidential and used in
aggregate for scientific purposes, avoiding undue identification of
individuals or organizations unless explicitly authorized.

**Voluntary participation**: your participation is very important and
voluntary, as it requires your approval for the use of the collected data.
Per Resolution CNS 466/2012 of the Brazilian National Health Council (CNS),
respect for human dignity requires that all research be conducted only with
free and informed consent. You have the right to decline or withdraw from
this study at any time, without penalty. If you decide to withdraw, please
notify the responsible researcher.

**Contact**: the researchers are available to provide clarification and
answer any questions.
- Researcher: Rafael Ribeiro Cardoso - rafaelcardoso.aluno@unipampa.edu.br
- Advisor: Prof. Dr. Maicon Bernardino da Silveira - bernardino@unipampa.edu.br
